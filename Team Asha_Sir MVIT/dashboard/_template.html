Section 01.1 ·

What Freezing of Gait Is

MDS-UPDRS Item 3.11
Kinematic transition profile L4/L5 tri-axial IMU
Bilateral stride cadence Alternating bilateral movement is arrested

The intention to walk continues. The stepping stops.

Freezing of Gait (FoG) is a brief, paroxysmal absence or marked reduction of forward stepping despite the explicit intention to walk. In advanced Parkinson's disease, episodes manifest as feet suddenly feeling "glued to the ground".

Motor planning remains intact, but striatal-cortical locomotor network breakdown arrests alternating bilateral movement, breaking kinetic rhythm unpredictably and producing sudden postural instability.

Transient triggers (turns, doorways, dual-tasking, fatigue) rarely manifest in a short, well-lit, unobstructed clinical hallway. This twin therefore works from passive, at-home movement recordings rather than clinic observation.

What this twin does not claim

  • Does not reproduce whole physiology, prevent falls, or guarantee an episode.
  • Does not forecast future burden; burden estimation is concurrent.
  • Does not claim external validation or clinical deployment.
  • Does not use the synthetic demonstration patient in any reported metric.
  • Does not substitute for clinical scoring or specialist examination.
  • Does not provide closed-loop stimulation commands or cues.
